Tasty Jellof Rice

The Secret to Perfect Jollof

With the following steps, you'll be a Jellof Rice cooking God! Extra Spicy 🔥

Ingredients

To prepare Jellof Rice, we first need the following ingredients:

  • Rice (The most important one 😝)
  • Tomatoes, Red Bell Peppers (Tatashe), and Scotch Bonnet (Atarodo)
  • Onions and Vegetable Oil
  • Tomato Paste
  • Seasoning cubes, Thyme, Curry powder, and Bay leaves

Preparation Steps

Now, let's head right into how to prepare a nice delicate plate of Jellof Rice:

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onions until smooth.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and fry some chopped onions. Add the tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.
  3. Pour in the blended tomato mixture and fry until the oil floats to the top (this is the stew base).
  4. Add water or meat stock, seasoning cubes, thyme, curry, and salt. Bring to a boil.
  5. Wash the rice thoroughly to remove excess starch.
  6. Add the washed rice to the boiling sauce. The liquid level should be just above the rice.
  7. Cover the pot tightly (use foil if possible) and cook on low heat until the rice is soft and the water is absorbed.
  8. Stir with a wooden spoon and serve hot!
